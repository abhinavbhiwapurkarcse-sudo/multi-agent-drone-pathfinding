- G. Sharon, R. Stern, A. Felner, N. Sturtevant. *Conflict-based search for optimal multi-agent pathfinding.* Artificial Intelligence 219, 40-66, 2015. (mentioned as an optimal alternative)
- R. Stern et al. *Multi-Agent Pathfinding: Definitions, Variants, and Benchmarks.* SoCS 2019. (the MovingAI benchmark set named in the task; **not used** in this repository, a self-generated grid is used instead)
